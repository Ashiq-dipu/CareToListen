Meet Maya & Marcus

We bring an investigative audio journalist and a trauma-informed licensed marriage and family therapist to the same microphones. Less performance, more presence.

CO-HOST & FOUNDER SHE / HER
RECORDING RIG:

Shure SM7B • Cloudlifter CL-1 • Universal Audio Apollo Twin X • Beyerdynamic DT 770 Pro

Maya Lin

Executive Audio Producer & Investigative Journalist

“True listening is not waiting for your turn to speak. It is creating a room where the other person finally feels safe enough to put down their luggage.”

Maya spent over a decade as an international investigative audio producer, reporting from refugee corridors and crisis centers across three continents. In 2021, after losing a close childhood friend to suicide, she realized that while the media excel at sensationalizing crisis, society gives grieving people almost nowhere to sit quietly with their pain.

She stepped away from breaking news to launch Care To Listen. Her interview style is unhurried, curious, and allergic to toxic positivity. She asks questions that linger in the room long after the tape stops rolling.

Three Studio Rituals

  • Cannot record an episode without a large ceramic mug of hot chamomile tea and oat milk.
  • Lives in Seattle with an adopted rescue greyhound named Echo who sleeps on the booth couch.
  • Reads at least three poetry collections every month to keep her emotional language precise.
MAYA'S ESSENTIAL LISTEN

Episode #108: Learning to Sit With the Silence

“It captured the exact essence of why I left fast-paced daily news: some stories are not headlines to be dissected, but human hearts to be held.”

CO-HOST & CLINICIAN HE / HIM
RECORDING RIG:

Electro-Voice RE20 • TritonAudio FetHead • Rødecaster Pro II • Sennheiser HD 650

Marcus Vance, LMFT

Licensed Marriage & Family Therapist • Somatic Lead

“People do not heal in isolation. We heal in the presence of someone whose eyes tell us: 'I see all of you, and I am not walking away.'”

Marcus is a licensed clinician with over twelve years in private practice and community mental health clinics, specializing in ambiguous loss, intergenerational trauma, and couples conflict.

On Care To Listen, Marcus provides the grounded clinical spine. He translates polyvagal research, internal family systems, and attachment theory into everyday human language, replacing cold diagnostic labels with warmth, validation, and practical somatic tools.

Three Studio Rituals

  • Former collegiate jazz bassist before training in clinical psychology and neuroscience.
  • Collects mid-century jazz vinyl and vintage mechanical wristwatches.
  • Believes 20 minutes of daily barefoot walking on earth resets any dysregulated nervous system.
MARCUS'S ESSENTIAL LISTEN

Episode #105: Navigating Sibling Estrangement

“Watching two estranged adult brothers lower their emotional armor in real time in our studio brought tears to everyone in the booth.”
