THE ETHOS A Sanctuary for Deep Presence

Why We Exist

We live in an era characterized by noise, algorithmic outrage, and quick-fix productivity hacks for grief. Care To Listen is an intentional slowing down.

ORIGIN NOTE

In 2021, we noticed that everywhere people turned for mental health, they were handed a 5-step checklist.

Grief was treated as an inefficiency to be optimized away. Loneliness was diagnosed as a personal failure of mindfulness.

Maya Lin, who had spent twelve years as an international investigative audio producer, met Marcus Vance, a licensed marriage and family therapist, in a Seattle community grief circle. They both realized the same truth: human beings do not heal because someone gave them clever advice. They heal because they were witnessed without judgment.

“When you sit in silence with another human being without attempting to fix them, you give them the rarest gift in the modern world: permission to be broken.”
BEHIND THE RECORDING DESK

The Three Studio Rituals

How we protect human nervous systems before, during, and after every recording session.

01

The 15-Minute Unrecorded Tea

We never start the tape immediately upon guest arrival. We sit in silence or drink warm tea to let heart rate variability settle after transit stress.

02

The Red Stop-Button Rule

Any guest can tap their microphone or hand-signal at any moment to pause the conversation. Retractions are unconditional and never argued.

03

Post-Session Somatic Decompression

Leaving a heavy recording session abruptly leaves adrenaline floating. Marcus guides a 5-minute somatic grounding sequence before anyone leaves the room.

EDITORIAL STANDARDS

Our Sanctuary Pledges

01

No Predatory Sponsors

We refuse ad money from gambling, speculative crypto, or venture-capital therapy platforms that sell listener behavioral data. We are 100% funded by our listeners and institutional grants.

02

Clinical Accuracy

Every clinical claim, polyvagal framework, or neurological reference is vetted against peer-reviewed journals before airtime. We bridge scientific rigor with open-hearted poetry.

03

Trauma-Informed Care

We never use sensationalist clickbait titles or trauma-porn teasers. Our studio guests have full editorial control to pause the tape or retract sensitive personal disclosures at any moment.

04

24/7 Lifeline Presence

We recognize that hearing deep conversations about grief can activate dormant pain. We embed verified, immediate regional crisis helplines into every episode transcript and page footer.
